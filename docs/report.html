MAKE THE UNKNOWN VISIBLE

A clean-looking dashboard
can hide a messy handover.

Proofboard checks declared fields, not the truth of a repair or a document. It cannot authenticate references, diagnose equipment, infer cash received, or verify that a person approved anything.

This prototype is a report checker, not a CRM or an AI agent. No customer messages or external actions are performed.
